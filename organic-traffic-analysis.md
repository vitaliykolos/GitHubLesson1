## Likely Causes of the June–August 2025 Organic Traffic Uptick

Based on research into the FiRa Consortium's publishing activity, the UWB industry landscape, and Google algorithm changes during that period, here are the most probable drivers:

### 1. Google June 2025 Core Update (June 30 – July 17)

This is likely the **single biggest factor**. Google rolled out its June 2025 core update from June 30 through July 17. Key characteristics of this update:

- It favored **authoritative, expert-driven content** with transparent authorship and credentials
- It triggered **partial recoveries** for sites that had been suppressed by prior updates (September 2023 HCU and earlier core updates)
- It created **high ranking volatility** with keyword swings of up to 20%
- The update expanded AI Overviews from ~10% to ~20% of queries

**Why this helped firaconsortium.org:** As the official industry consortium for UWB technology — a non-profit with 100+ member companies including NXP, Samsung, Bosch, and ASSA ABLOY — FiRa's site signals extremely high E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). Google's June core update likely rewarded this authority, boosting rankings for UWB-related queries.

### 2. FiRa Blog Post: "How UWB is Reshaping the Global Economy" (June 5, 2025)

This blog post covered a major **global economic study** commissioned by FiRa and conducted by Telecom Advisory Services, revealing:
- UWB will generate **$36.8 billion** in global socio-economic value by 2030 (up from $10.5B in 2024)
- Over **1.5 billion UWB-enabled devices** had shipped by end of 2023
- Projected **1.4 billion devices shipped annually** by 2029

This type of data-rich, industry-defining content attracts backlinks and search traffic from journalists, analysts, and industry professionals searching for UWB market data.

### 3. Surging UWB Industry Interest (Summer 2025)

Multiple events drove increased search interest in UWB technology during this period:

- **Samsung Galaxy Unpacked (July 2025)** — Samsung's flagship launch event drives search interest in Samsung-adjacent technologies including UWB
- **Google expanding UWB in "Find My Device"** — Google confirmed UWB would be added to its device-finding feature, with 4x faster location speeds. This generated significant tech press coverage
- **Apple AirTag refresh rumors** — Anticipated second-gen AirTag with updated UWB chip drove consumer and tech media interest
- **FiRa Spectrum Position Statement update (July 2025)** — FiRa published an updated position paper on preserving the 7.7–8.3 GHz band for UWB
- **FiRa Board Officer announcements (August 26, 2025)** — New leadership announcements generated industry press coverage
- **UWB market projections** widely cited — Multiple research firms (MarketsandMarkets, Mordor Intelligence, Straits Research) published bullish UWB market forecasts, driving search queries about UWB standards and certification

### 4. Content Publishing Cadence

FiRa maintained a steady publishing cadence in the months leading up to and during the uptick:
- January 2025: FiRa Core 3.0 Specifications launch (major press release)
- March 2025: omlox partnership announcement
- April 2025: Blog post
- May 2025: Blog post; Infineon joins FiRa board
- June 2025: "How UWB is Reshaping the Global Economy" blog post
- July 2025: Spectrum Position Statement
- August 2025: Board Officer announcements

This consistent output gave Google fresh content to index and likely improved crawl frequency.

## Strategy to Reach 1,000+ Organic Visits/Month

### Quick Wins (1–2 months)

1. **Expand the economic impact content** — The $36.8B study is a link magnet. Create derivative content:
   - "UWB Market Size 2025: Key Statistics" (targets "UWB market size" queries)
   - "UWB Use Cases by Industry" (targets long-tail informational queries)
   - Infographic summarizing the study data (shareable, earns backlinks)

2. **Optimize existing high-potential pages** — In Ahrefs, find pages ranking positions 4–20 and improve:
   - `/certifications/certification-program` — optimize for "UWB certification"
   - `/about/consortium` — optimize for "FiRa Consortium" and "what is FiRa"
   - `/certifications/certified-devices` — optimize for "UWB certified devices"
   - Add FAQ schema markup to these pages for rich snippets

3. **Target "UWB vs. Bluetooth" comparison queries** — High search volume topic where FiRa has natural authority. A detailed comparison guide could capture significant traffic.

### Medium-Term Growth (3–6 months)

4. **Build a UWB knowledge hub / glossary** — Create a comprehensive resource targeting educational queries:
   - "What is ultra-wideband"
   - "How does UWB work"
   - "UWB vs BLE vs NFC"
   - "UWB range and accuracy"
   These informational queries have consistent volume and establish topical authority.

5. **Content gap analysis vs. competitors** — In Ahrefs Content Gap, compare firaconsortium.org against:
   - pozyx.io (UWB positioning company with active blog)
   - decawave.com / qorvo.com (UWB chipmakers)
   - csa-iot.org (Connectivity Standards Alliance, FiRa collaborator)
   Find keywords they rank for that FiRa doesn't.

6. **Leverage member news for SEO** — When members like Samsung, Apple, NXP, or STMicroelectronics make UWB announcements, publish timely commentary/analysis on the FiRa blog. This captures news-driven search traffic.

### Ongoing

7. **Publishing cadence** — Maintain 2–4 blog posts per month targeting keyword opportunities identified in Ahrefs
8. **Backlink strategy** — Pitch the economic study data to tech journalists and industry analysts; offer expert commentary for UWB-related articles
9. **Technical SEO** — Run Ahrefs Site Audit to catch crawl errors, broken links, slow pages; ensure all blog posts have proper meta titles/descriptions
10. **Track progress** — Set up Ahrefs Rank Tracker for top 50 target keywords; monitor monthly
